The Descrition for Activity Diagram - Money Exchange

- The process begins when the customer registers an account.
- The system creates the customer record and checks whether the customer already exists.
- If the customer is new, the system creates a new user account.
- The customer enters the exchange country.
- The customer enters the recipient details.
- The customer enters the sending amount.
- The system gets the current exchange rate.
- The system creates an exchange request.
- The customer reviews and accepts the exchange rate.
- The customer enters their credit card details.
- The system processes the exchange transaction.
- The system updates the exchange status.
- The process ends after the exchange status has been successfully updated.
